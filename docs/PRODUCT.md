# Product directive

AudioGraffiti is a mobile-first thought capture tool with a synchronized desktop workspace. Its premise must be immediately understandable to a LinkedIn visitor:

**Capture a thought now. Find it when you're ready. Give it time if it needs it.**

A thought is not an unfinished task. It can stay a thought indefinitely. Thoughts are purple until a follow-up date and time is applied, then amber. Removing the follow-up returns them to purple. The original capture timestamp does not affect their color. “Follow up on this idea tomorrow” places the thought on the calendar without converting it into a green to-do. Type, icon, and label accompany the color.

There are two calendars:
- **Thoughts & To-dos:** time to revisit an idea or work on an action.
- **Events:** appointments and fixed commitments.

Both calendars contribute to availability. An AI suggestion is never a confirmed commitment until approved.

Phone entry opens directly to Capture. Speak and type/paste are both immediately accessible. No project, category, AI conversation, or review is required before or after saving. Stopping the recording saves it. Dark mode is the default for nighttime use.

Desktop entry opens Today. It shows thoughts since the previous review, suggestions, ideas, questions, priorities, and the day's schedule. Review suggestions individually or collectively; retain originals. Manual tasks, thoughts, notes, projects, and events work without AI. Projects are optional organization, not a required workflow.

Use a single private account on every device. Clearly distinguish “saved on this device,” uploading, queued, transcribing, organizing, ready, and failed. Never call a local-only thought synchronized.

## Mandatory scenario

Capture three separate thoughts on a phone overnight. Open the desktop in the morning. All successfully submitted captures appear, either processed or with an explicit status. Context can connect to existing thoughts, tasks, projects and calendar blocks. Ideas are preserved without automatic conversion. No manual transfer or reentry.

This is MVP behavior, not a future enhancement. See ACCEPTANCE.md for verification and remaining real-device/cloud gates.
